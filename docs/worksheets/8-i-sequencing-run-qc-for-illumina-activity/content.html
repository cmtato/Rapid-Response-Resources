Activity: Basespace or SAV Illumina Run Review

Hint: use the “Sequencing Run QC for Illumina” as a guide

  1. Look at Clusters Pass Filter and Average Q30. How did the run look, overall? Was this a good / okay / bad sequencing run?





  1. How much data did you get back (reads)? How does this compare to what you expected?

Hint: Google your sequencing kit to find expected output





  1. Which metric(s) will you look at to check your loading concentration?

Hint: Look at % aligned

Hint: Does the sequencer used use a patterned or non-patterned flow cell?

Non-patterned: look at density; patterned look at % occupied x % PF



    1. Do you think enough library pool was loaded?




    1. Next time would you load more, less, or the same amount?




  1. What is another way to look at clustering? How did it look for this run? Over-, under-, just right?

Hint: Thumbnail images (available for some sequencers)






  1. How did the library composition look? What metric/chart will you look at to evaluate this? Do you see evidence of adapter dimer? Do you have short fragments?

Hint: % Base chart





  1. How was the index distribution and how does this compare to the expected based on sample pooling? Did your group’s samples receive equal/expected percentage of reads?






  1. If you got 100% of your reads labeled as “Undetermined”, what issue do you suspect? How would you correct this?







  1. If you do not have the sequencer connected to the internet or Basespace, what tool can you use to look at your run metrics?

Hint: Sequencing Analysis Viewer (SAV)





  1. Based on your answers above, how would you improve your next sequencing run?
