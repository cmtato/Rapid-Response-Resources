5. SPRI Bead Clean-up and Size Selection
SPRI bead ratio: 0.9

Sample volume (per full rxn): 100.00

SPRI to add (per rxn): 90.00

Elution volume (per rxn): 22

Aspiration volume (per rxn): 20

SPRI clean-up protocol:

*allow beads to sit in RT for 30 mins prior. Ensure you have enough 80% ethanol.

1. Add room temperature beads to rxn. Mix well by pipetting up and down 10 times.
2. Incubate for 5 mins at room temperature.
3. Place samples on magnetic rack, and incubate for 5 mins on the rack.
4. Remove supernatant
5. Add 200uL of 80% EtOH to samples while on the magnetic rack. Do not disturb beads.
6. Incubate at room temperature for 30s and remove the supernatant.
7. Repeat EtOH wash step once for a total of 2 washes.
8. Remove any excess EtOH with a p10 or p20 pipette.
9. Air dry the beads for 2-5 mins while on the magnetic rack.
Watch beads carefully throughout this incubation to avoid overdrying. Smaller bead pellets dry faster. Trust your eyes more than the timer.
10. Remove tube from magnetic rack. Add desired elution volume of nuclease-free water.
11. Pipette to mix well and incubate for 2 mins at room temperature off the magnetic rack.
12. Pulse spin tubes and place on magnetic rack until solution is clear, ~ 5 mins.
13. Aspirate supernatant and transfer to a clean nuclease free PCR tube.

**Checkpoint: Samples can be stored frozen at -20°C and library prep resumed the next day.
