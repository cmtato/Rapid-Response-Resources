4. SPRI Clean after Barcoding PCR



* Follow bead clean protocol in manufacturer's protocol (and briefly 1-19. below)



** Prepare 400ul fresh 80% EtOH per sample prior to beginning



1. Place samples on the magnet
2. Transfer 45ul of supernatant to a fresh tube
3. Add 45ul IPB beads to each well
4. Mix well
5. Incubate at RT for 5 min
6. Place on magnet for 5 min
7. Remove and discard 90ul of supernatant
8. Keep on magnet and add 175ul fresh 80% EtOH
9. Wait 30s, then remove and discard EtOH
10. Repeat steps 8-9 for a second ethanol wash
11. With a 20ul pipette, remove all residual EtOH
12. Air-dry on magnet for 2min - do not over-dry the beads
13. Remove from the magnet
14. Add 32ul RSB or water to each well
15. Mix well by pipetting or seal and shake
16. Incubate at RT for 2min
17. Pulse spin
18. Place on magnet for 2min
19. Transfer 30ul supernatant to fresh tube
