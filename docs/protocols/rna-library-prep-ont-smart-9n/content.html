5. SPRI Bead Clean-up
SPRI bead ratio: 0.6


Sample volume (full rxn): 50


SPRI to add (per full rxn): 30 µL

Elution volume (per full rxn): 17 µL

Aspiration volume (per full rxn): 15 µL


SPRI clean-up protocol:

*allow beads to sit in RT for 30 mins prior. Ensure you have enough 80% ethanol (500ul per sample).

1. Add room temperature beads to rxn. Mix well by pipetting up and down 10 times.
2. Incubate for 5 mins at room temperature.
3. Place samples on magnetic rack, and incubate for 5 mins on the rack.
4. Remove supernatant and add 200uL of 80% EtOH to samples while on the magnetic rack.
5. Incubate at room temperature for 30s and remove the supernatant.
6. Repeat EtOH wash step for a total of 2 times.
7. Air dry the beads for up to 3-5 mins while on the magnetic rack. Watch beads carefully throughout this incubation to avoid overdrying; smaller bead pellets dry faster. Trust your eyes more than the timer.
8. Remove tube from magnetic rack. Elute DNA from beads into nuclease free water (can also use 0.1x TE Buffer, 10mM Tris-HCl)
9. Pipette mix and incubate for a minimum of 5 mins at room temperature off the magnetic rack.
10. Place on magnetic rack until solution is clear, ~ 5 mins.
11. Aspirate supernatant and transfer to a clean nuclease free PCR tube.

**Checkpoint: Samples can be stored frozen at -20°C and library prep resumed the next day.
6. QC of individual libraries
Qubit dsDNA HS: ng/ul concentration* of each sample

Tapestation, Genomic DNA: nM concentration* and size distribution of each sample

Nanodrop dsDNA (optional): identify potential contaminants that may affect pore health

* If individual libraries are too low concentration at this stage, consider moving to step 5.5. Secondary Amplification
7. Pooling
Pool all barcoded libraries in the desired ratios to a total of 100-150 fmol in 11 µL of water (~10-15nM) or elution buffer (EB). See Pooling Calculation Worksheet and NEB calculator: https://nebiocalculator.neb.com/#!/dsdnaamt Complete QC (Qubit) of pooled libraries. Pool can be stored at -20C at this stage.

Secondary amplification may be warranted if there is not enough material to sequence following initial PCR Amplification

How do I determine whether I have sufficient material?
- Target concentration for each sample is 5-15nM. 5-15nM is not required for every sample, but you'll want most samples to be around this concentration
- Tapestation trace may help you evaluate if you have enough library
- Qubit is most reliable for concentration measurement
